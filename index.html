Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Please review our cancellation policy below before booking.

Booking Deposit

  • A booking deposit of 50% of the total stay cost is required to confirm your reservation. This deposit is non-refundable under all circumstances, including cancellations covered by the policy below.

Cancellation Refunds

  • 14 or more days before check-in: full refund of all payments made, excluding the non-refundable booking deposit.
  • 7–13 days before check-in: 50% refund of the total booking cost, excluding the non-refundable booking deposit.
  • Less than 7 days before check-in: no refund.
  • No-shows or early departures: no refund for unused nights.

How to Cancel

All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email to info@21devalle.co.za. The cancellation date will be recorded as the date we receive your written notice.

Date Changes

If you need to change your dates rather than cancel, please contact us as early as possible. We will do our best to accommodate a rebooking free of charge, subject to availability, if the request is made 7 or more days before your original check-in date.

Peak Season / High-Demand Dates

For bookings over public holidays, festivals, and other high-demand periods, a stricter policy applies:

  • 30 or more days before check-in: full refund, excluding the non-refundable booking deposit.
  • 14–29 days before check-in: 50% refund.
  • Less than 14 days before check-in: no refund.

Circumstances Beyond Your Control

We recognise that unforeseen events, such as serious illness, injury, or government travel restrictions, can affect travel plans. In these cases, please contact us directly with supporting documentation. We will consider such requests on a case-by-case basis and may offer a partial refund, credit toward a future stay, or a date change at our discretion.

Host-Initiated Cancellations

In the rare event that we must cancel your booking (e.g., due to property issues beyond our control), we will notify you as soon as possible and provide a full refund of all payments made, including the booking deposit.
